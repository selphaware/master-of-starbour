## 5. Numbers the build must hit (default ini)

Universe diameter 8 × 16 = 128 flattened sectors → placement distance ≥ 64. Eligibility:
2,000 silver-equivalent = 100 gold; fear and respect ≥ 90; 3 distinct of the 4 Mythical
elements. Guardians: 4 × Bastion strength 445 = 1,780 (M4 § 5 formula) against a
maxed Kestrel at ~100 + 99 × 15 + 10 × 5 × 3 = 1,735 — a maxed solo ship is roughly
even with the guard, an un-upgraded one is not: the endgame expects a fleet or
upgrades, and `guard_ships` is the knob. Deep space: 20 % of galaxy cells are Void
(CFG-63) → ~51 deep-space sectors per galaxy, ~13 extra stacks per galaxy at 25 %, and
~15 % of roaming AI in deep space at any time.

## 6. Known traps

- **Deep-space RNG.** Derive it from `(seed, coordinates)`; touching the world or
  in-game RNG changes every later roll and breaks D-67 (smoke/49 will show it as a
  differing `voids` listing).
- **The echo is not an order.** `command.echo` is logged and dropped; it must never
  reach the order queue or change a snapshot hash.
- **Mastery locks live in the engine**, not the terminals: deltas, deductions and
  consumption are skipped at the source so the report proves it, not the display.
- **The ceremony is T3's business.** The engine emits one event; T3 animates between
  redraws and must keep consuming `turn.resolved`.
- **Guardians are AI captains** for the bus and the snapshot, but not for the
  population bound or roaming; keep the flag in one place.
- **Width.** `help` renders at the live console width; the 200-column buffer was the
  bug.
- **Do not weaken a gate.**
