## 5. Numbers the build must hit (default ini)

Disposition: respect 10 → −80, 50 → 0, 100 → +100. Planet buy factor at respect 50
= 0.90, at 100 = 0.80; sell factor 1.10 / 1.20. Food: 10 units at respect 50 = 2 ×
10 × 0.9 = 18 silver. Iridium sold at respect 50 = 30 × 0.6 × 1.1 = 19.8 → 19 silver
(floor). Barter threshold at respect 10 (disposition −80): received ≥ given × 1.16;
at respect 90 (+80): ≥ given × 0.84. Kestrel 40 gold; starting captain has 5 gold +
50 silver = 150 silver-equivalent, so a ship needs ~26 gold more. Planet strength (torpedo_damage 15 since Chat A32) = 80 + 10×15 + 4×5×3 = 290 (vs Kestrel 60 + 6×15 + 45 = 195 → ratio 1.49 ≥ 1.3: a low-respect Kestrel in range gets shot). Corsair 305 / Kestrel 195 = 1.56, Bastion 445 / 195 = 2.28 — the M3 AI-opens rows still hold.

## 6. Known traps

- **Trade resolves at TP-05, after movement and combat.** A `buy` queued on a planet
  you then leave in the same turn is rejected at resolution ("not in the planet's
  cell"), not at submission.
- **Floors.** Every price is floored to whole silver after the factor; gold converts
  only when silver is short (`silver_per_gold`), never upward automatically.
- **Planet removal** must leave the sector's terrain consistent (the cell becomes
  Void), update `world_index`, fog-of-war visited flags and any AI restock target
  pointing at it.
- **AI restock trips must respect hazards and ranges** (D-103) and never target a
  planet that refuses the AI (its own respect).
- **Population bound** is the gate for the whole AI economy; if it fails, tune
  CFG-81 in the *gate's* temp ini reasoning first — if the defaults can't hold the
  bound, report it as a balance finding rather than changing the bound.
- **FR-07 fires once per accepted trade**, not per unit; `buy` and `sell` count as
  trades (TR-09 "any accepted trade").
- **Do not weaken a gate.**
