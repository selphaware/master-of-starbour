### 4a. Conventions the gates settled (D-128, D-129) — the build must honour these

- `spawned` (C10) sits at the report's top level (turn 0), or in `per_turn[0]`.
- A script order the engine rejects appears as a C2 event string containing `rejected` with the reason (smoke/24 reads the out-of-range reason there).
- An AI ship in a battle holds its cell unless its EF-02 rule says escape, so a spawn distance is the exchange distance. When several EF-02 rules apply at once: surrender > escape > peace.
- `warps[]` lists AI warps too (smoke/34 counts roams from it and `final.roams`).
- The T4 status heartbeat (C5) carries `in_battle`.
- The launcher forwards `--spawn` and `--ai` to the engine (as it does `--teleport`).
- Peace X (D-46) is judged on the victor/loser shield numbers the report shows for the turn the battle ended.
- `starbour/config.py` must accept the degenerate class values the gates' temp inis use: `engine 0`, `phasers 0`, `torpedoes 0` and `600`, `shield_max 1`, `torpedo_damage 0.1` — validation rejects only negatives, percentages outside 0–100 and distances below 1.

## 6. Known traps

- **Range after movement, not before.** Battle participation and fire validity use
  TP-02 end positions; a `fire` queued at distance 1 whose target moves to 6 misses
  the exchange with an `order.rejected`-style event, not a crash.
- **Simultaneity.** Compute every hit from the state at the start of the weapon
  phase, then apply in ship-id order; destruction is checked after each landing hit
  (HZ-09), so order matters only for who gets the loot (D-123).
- **Ratios at open.** Store `strength_at_open` on the battle; never recompute it for
  FR-03…05 or LV-03.
- **AI-vs-AI loot.** CB-01 applies between AI captains too; captains with no ships
  are removed the same turn (D-105), and their open battles end.
- **Captured ships keep their queues empty** and their `captain` field changes; the
  AI must not keep issuing orders to a ship it no longer owns.
- **Roaming must respect reach** (MV-02) and `effective = w − pull` (HZ-10); an AI
  that warps from inside a hole's range fails like the player does.
- **Game over mid-exchange**: if the player's active ship dies while another owned
  ship survives, it is still game over (FL-04, D-45) — the captain is on the active
  ship.
- **Do not weaken a gate.** If smoke/30's thresholds cannot be met with the default
  ini, that is a finding about CFG-29/30/72 — report it.
